#### Summer Night Dock Light Pattern

Source: Personal observation / reading
Confidence: 3

Warm water pushes ethical striper fishing toward low-light or night windows.
Dock lights can concentrate plankton, threadfin shad, and feeding predators.

Tags:

Potential pattern:

- Water temp: 75-82+
- Light: night / dock light
- Structure: dock light near point or channel
- Technique: fly retrieve, fluke, topwater edges, live bait
